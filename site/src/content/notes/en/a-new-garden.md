This website is starting again.

It will hold four kinds of content: updates, notes, bookmarks, and projects. They don’t all need to be equally polished, or arrive on the same schedule.

## Updates: write it down first

A sentence, an idea I’m working through, or a small step forward on a project. If it deserves more space later, it can become a note.

## Notes: think it through

Notes make room for the longer process. I can return to add something, change my mind, or connect a new thread.

## Bookmarks: keep the reason, too

Alongside each link, I want to leave a reason for saving it. When I come back, I’ll remember what caught my attention.

## Projects: let the work speak

A record of what I’m building, the problems it addresses, and how it’s actually progressing.

This is a newly cleared patch of ground. The first note starts here.
